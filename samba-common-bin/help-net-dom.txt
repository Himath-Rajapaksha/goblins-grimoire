Usage:
net dom join <domain=DOMAIN> <ou=OU> <account=ACCOUNT> <password=PASSWORD> <reboot>
  Join a remote machine
net dom unjoin <account=ACCOUNT> <password=PASSWORD> <reboot>
  Unjoin a remote machine
net dom renamecomputer <newname=NEWNAME> <account=ACCOUNT> <password=PASSWORD> <reboot>
  Rename joined computer
