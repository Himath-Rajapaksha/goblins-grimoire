
Usage: 
  net lookup [host] HOSTNAME[#<type>]
	gives IP for a hostname

  net lookup ldap [domain] [sitename]
	gives IP of domain's ldap server

  net lookup kdc [realm]
	gives IP of realm's kerberos KDC

  net lookup pdc [domain|realm]
	gives IP of realm's kerberos KDC

  net lookup dc [domain]
	gives IP of domains Domain Controllers

  net lookup master [domain|wg]
	give IP of master browser

  net lookup name [name]
	Lookup name's sid and type

  net lookup sid [sid]
	Give sid's name and type

  net lookup dsgetdcname [name] [flags] [sitename]
