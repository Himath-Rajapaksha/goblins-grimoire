Usage:
net sam createbuiltingroup
    Create a new BUILTIN group
net sam createlocalgroup
    Create a new local group
net sam createdomaingroup
    Create a new group
net sam deletelocalgroup
    Delete an existing local group
net sam deletedomaingroup
    Delete a group
net sam mapunixgroup
    Map a unix group to a domain group
net sam unmapunixgroup
    Remove a group mapping of an unix group to a domain group
net sam addmem
    Add a member to a group
net sam delmem
    Delete a member from a group
net sam listmem
    List group members
net sam list
    List users, groups and local groups
net sam show
    Show details of a SAM entry
net sam set
    Set details of a SAM account
net sam policy
    Set account policies
net sam rights
    Manipulate user privileges
net sam privison
    Provision a clear user database
